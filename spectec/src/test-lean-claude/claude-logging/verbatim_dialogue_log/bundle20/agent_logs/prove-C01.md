### `t_progress_be_block` : proved
Still-`sorry` earlier lemmas relied on: `lookup_types`.
```lean
  intro C bt bes vt1 vt2 HBok HType HWfC HWfinstr HWfC' IHH
  unfold t_progress_be_P
  intro s f C' vcs ts1 ts2 lab ret HWfConfig HWfVals Htf Hcontext Hmod Hts Hstore Hnotbr Hnotret
  right
  refine ⟨s, f, [admininstr.LABEL_ vt2.length [] (List.map admininstr_val vcs ++ List.map admininstr_instr bes)], ?_⟩
  simp only [mkFunctype, functype.mk_functype.injEq, list.mk_list.injEq] at Htf
  obtain ⟨Htf1, _⟩ := Htf
  rw [← Htf1] at Hts
  have Hfun : fun_blocktype (state.mk_state s f) bt = functype.mk_functype (list.mk_list vt1) (list.mk_list vt2) := by
    cases HBok with
    | valtype valtype_opt _ _ => cases valtype_opt <;> rfl
    | typeidx x _ _ _ Hty _ _ =>
      show f.MODULE.TYPES[proj_uN_0 x]! = _
      rw [← lookup_types s f C' (List.map typeof f.LOCALS) lab ret _ Hmod, ← Hcontext]
      exact Hty
  have Hlen : vcs.length = vt1.length := by rw [← Hts, List.length_map]
  exact Step.read _ _ _ (Step_read.block (state.mk_state s f) vcs.length vcs bt bes vt2.length
    vt1 vt2 Hfun rfl Hlen rfl)
```

Notes:
- `nop`/`drop`/`select_*`/`block`: `Htf : functype.mk_functype (list.mk_list ..) (list.mk_list ..) = mkFunctype ts1 ts2`
  is split with `simp only [mkFunctype, functype.mk_functype.injEq, list.mk_list.injEq]` (Rocq `injection`/`case: Htf`).
- `select_*`: a `match vcs, Hts, HWfVals with | [v1, v2, v3], .. =>` was tried first and failed (the equation compiler
  cannot refute the other lengths through `List.map typeof vcs = [t, t, I32]`); `rcases` on the first four
  conses + `simp at Hts` for impossible lengths works. Rocq `destruct v3; try discriminate; injection` is
  `cases v3` + `simp [admininstr_val] at Hn3` for the non-CONST constructors; Rocq `case: n3` is `cases n3`.
- `block`: `Blocktype_ok`'s context `C` is promoted to an inductive parameter, so constructor patterns are
  `valtype valtype_opt _ _` and `typeidx x _ _ _ Hty _ _` (no slot for C). In the `typeidx` case,
  `fun_blocktype (mk_state s f) (_IDX x)` is `f.MODULE.TYPES[proj_uN_0 x]!` by `show` (defeq), and Rocq's
  `erewrite <- lookup_types` is `rw [← lookup_types s f C' (List.map typeof f.LOCALS) lab ret _ Hmod, ← Hcontext]`.
  The IH (`IHH`) is unused, as in Rocq.
